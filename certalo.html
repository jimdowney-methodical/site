Introducing Certalo

Certalo — CMMC self-assessment, compressed from months into days

Certalo is the AI CMMC assistant built for the Defense Industrial Base. It works from the documents and evidence you provide about your environment — drafting your System Security Plan, scoring SPRS as you go, and preparing the policy and procedure documentation an assessor expects — so a self-assessment that used to take months of manual spreadsheet work takes days.

What Certalo Does

Your CMMC assessment, guided end to end

Certalo covers the full NIST SP 800-171 Rev 2 self-assessment lifecycle — from evidence to SSP to a posted SPRS score — with an AI assistant doing the heavy documentation work alongside you.

AI-drafted System Security Plan

Certalo turns the evidence and documents you upload into an SSP mapped to all 110 controls — no blank-page spreadsheets. You review and refine; it writes the first draft.

Live SPRS scoring

Your SPRS score updates in real time as you assess. See exactly which unmet requirements cost you 5, 3, or 1 points — and which fixes move the number most.

Guided 800-171A walkthrough

Work every determination statement across the 14 families and 320 assessment objectives, record findings with linked evidence, and flag POA&M-eligible gaps.

POA&M that stays current

Generate a Plan of Action & Milestones from your open gaps — owners, milestones, target dates — and keep it live as your environment and evidence change.

Policy & procedure drafting

An AI assistant that analyzes the documents describing your environment and drafts the policy and procedure documentation assessors expect — tailored to how your organization operates.

SPRS-ready submission package

Certalo lays out the exact field values you enter at SPRS and bundles the supporting artifacts — score, SSP, POA&M, and assessment report — with copy-ready fields, so posting your score is fast and accurate.

Pre-C3PAO Readiness Checklist

30 questions to answer before you engage a Certified Third-Party Assessment Organization. Work through each category to identify gaps before they cost you the assessment.

How to use this checklist: Answer each question honestly based on your current state — not your intended future state. A "No" answer is a gap that needs a remediation plan documented in your POA&M before you schedule your C3PAO assessment. C3PAOs will verify your claims with evidence; prepare accordingly.

Your progress is saved in this browser session. Check off items as you confirm them. Use the results to prioritize your pre-assessment remediation work.
